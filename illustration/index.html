An illustrated picture book that encourages children to discover recycling through playful characters who turn old things into something new.

01The book

Five characters in a talking recycling bin

Skräphjältarna ("The Trash Heroes") follows five characters who live inside a talking recycling bin: Magical Metal, Glittery Glass, Peppy Plastic, Cuddly Cardboard and Cool Compost. Each one shows children how their kind of waste gets a second life.

The book was created in collaboration with the Rinkeby-Kista district administration in Stockholm, aimed at inspiring children and adults to sort their waste. It's printed on Nordic Swan-certified recycled paper.
